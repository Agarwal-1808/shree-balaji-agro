01 / OUR STORY

Rooted in tradition.
Growing with purpose.

A story that began in Jharkhand.

Since 2010, Shree Balaji Agro Farm Private Limited has been part of the region’s rice manufacturing story. In 2025, we expanded our range to include atta, maida and sooji.

Shree Bhog is our consumer product brand, manufactured by Shree Balaji Agro Farm Private Limited. Together, our rice and flour range serves homes, retailers and business partners.
